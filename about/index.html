01 / ORIGIN
How CyX_9D
Came to Be

CyX_9D started in a dorm room in 2024 with a simple frustration: every resource for learning ethical hacking and other tech skills was either scattered across a thousand forums, locked behind expensive certifications, or written in a way that assumed you already knew everything.

The founder — a self-taught penetration tester and a growing Engineering student who broke into hacking, tech and engineering through sheer stubbornness — wanted to build the resource he wished existed when he started. Not a certification course. Not a YouTube channel. Something deeper, more structured, and completely free.

Two years later, CyX_9D has grown into a platform covering everything from Linux and hacking fundamentals to computer programming & development — with writeups, tool guides, AI/ML security research, Maths & Science full foundational course, and roadmaps that takes you from zero to elite without spending a single dollar on paywalls.

The mission hasn't changed. Ethical hacking knowledge and others, all belongs to everyone.

02 / MEANING
What does
CyX_9D mean

CyX_9D is a technology-focused learning and building ecosystem built around one idea: there is more than one dimension to understanding technology.

The CyX identity represents the core — curiosity, systems thinking, experimentation, and the drive to understand how things work.

The _9D represents Nine Dimensions: a broader view of technology spanning areas such as:
Code · CyberSec · Intelligence · Robotics · Engineering · Systems · Automation · Research · Creation

Together, CyX_9D represents a space where these disciplines connect rather than exist in isolation.

The name reflects what the platform is becoming: not simply a collection of tutorials, but an environment for learning, experimenting, building, and connecting ideas across different technical domains.

Why “9D”?
Because technology doesn't exist in a single dimension.
Programming connects to systems.
Systems connect to engineering.
Engineering connects to robotics.
AI connects to intelligence and automation.
Cybersecurity connects to almost everything.

The boundaries overlap.

That is the idea behind _9D — exploring technology from multiple dimensions and learning how those dimensions work together.

03 / VALUES
What We
Stand For

Everything we build, write, and teach is grounded in these four principles. They are not aspirational — they are operational constraints.

⬡
Radical Accessibility
Every lesson, writeup, and tool guide is free. No premium tier, no paywalled basics. If we can't give it away, we don't publish it.
⬡
Technical Honesty
We do not water down techniques or skip the hard parts. If something is complex, we explain it fully — not just hand you a one-liner to copy.
⬡
Ethical First
Every technique we teach & do exists to make systems more secure. We are penetration testers, not criminals. Context and consent are non-negotiable.
⬡
Community Driven
CyX_9D grows through contributions. Real writeups from real engagements, not theoretical examples. The community is the product.

08 / FAQ
Common
Questions

Straight answers. No marketing copy.

Is CyX_9D really completely free? +

Yes — completely, unconditionally free. No premium tier, no paywalled articles, no "starter plan." Every writeup, every roadmap lesson, every tool guide is accessible without an account. Creating an account only adds progress tracking and bookmarks.

Do I need any prior experience to start? +

No. Any article or Phase 1 of any roadmap starts from zero — Linux command line, networking fundamentals, and setting up a lab. If you can use a computer and you're willing to spend time in a terminal, you can start from the beginning and work your way up.

Is the content actually ethical? Am I learning to hack illegally? +

Everything here, and every technique taught on CyX_9D is for use on systems you own, have explicit permission to test, or on intentional practice environments (HackTheBox, TryHackMe, VulnHub). We cover the legal and ethical framework in Phase 1. Unauthorised access to systems is illegal everywhere — we teach skills, not crimes.

Can I contribute a writeup or post? +

Absolutely. We actively want community contributions — CTF writeups, tool guides, technique breakdowns. If you've learned something the hard way and want to save others the frustration, reach out via Email or the Contact us page. All contributors are credited.

How often is new content published? +

The core team publishes 2–4 pieces per week. Between writeups, tool guides, roadmap updates, and CTF walkthroughs, there's consistently new content. Subscribe to the newsletter if you don't want to miss anything.

Will CyX_9D ever go paid or add ads? +

No ads, ever. The platform is currently self-funded. If we ever pursue sustainability through revenue, it will be through optional merchandise or voluntary support — not by locking content or injecting ads into the reading experience. That commitment is in writing.
